# Screenshot evidence checklist
Capture screenshots from your own running application after starting MongoDB, backend and frontend. Suggested files:
1. `01-login.png` — login and registration view.
2. `02-admin-dashboard.png` — admin login and overview.
3. `03-student-dashboard.png` — student subject catalogue and quiz sets.
4. `04-quiz-question.png` — a question with selected option.
5. `05-quiz-result.png` — automatically evaluated score.
6. `06-mongodb-attempts.png` — saved Attempt documents (hide personal data).
7. `07-network-devtools.png` — browser Network tab showing request/response.
8. `08-fsm-and-transition-table.png` — include the FLAT diagram and table in report.
Do not submit mock screenshots as proof of execution; capture genuine output from your local installation.
